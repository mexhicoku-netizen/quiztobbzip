Privacy

Short version: your quiz never leaves your computer.

Your files

When you drop a quiz or key into this page, it is read and converted by JavaScript running in your own browser. It is not uploaded, sent to a server, stored, or shown to any AI service. The downloads are created in your browser too. Closing the tab discards everything.

The site has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics scripts of its own.

Cookies and local storage

The converter itself sets no cookies and stores nothing. If you choose “Accept ads” in the banner, your choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage so the banner does not ask again; “Cookie settings” in the footer lets you change it.

Advertising

This site shows ads from Google AdSense to cover its running costs. Ads load only after you accept them. When they do, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, and may personalise them based on your browsing. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services and Google’s ad settings. If you decline, no ad scripts are loaded. None of this ever involves the content of your quizzes, which stay in your browser.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by its hosting provider, which, like any web host, may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability. That is outside this site’s control.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact details on the About page.
